# Milestone 5 status — 2026-08-09

Milestone 5's P1 playback and queue scope is complete. Sleep timer UI was deliberately removed from scope. Fullscreen and the optional spectrum are now delivered; the compact mini-player and cached waveform remain P2 work.

## Delivered

- Named bookmarks can be created, renamed, opened, and removed. Automatic track-position resume can be toggled, and bookmark data survives database recovery.
- Speed, pitch, pitch preservation, reset, and per-track processing overrides are available from the playback menu.
- Stop after current and Stop after queue are mutually exclusive, persist across launches, suppress predecode at the boundary, and clear themselves after completion.
- Track rows and the persistent player expose 0–5 star rating, click-again-to-clear, and Love remains available.
- The seek bar shows hover time, chapters, and visually distinct bookmark markers.
- Queue editing supports extended selection, batch removal, move to top/bottom, Ctrl+Up/Down reordering, drag insertion feedback, Ctrl+Z/Ctrl+Y, and visible undo/redo notices.
- Queue history is session-persisted, and queue presentation distinguishes CURRENT, NEXT, and LATER.
- Shuffle uses a stable deck, avoids immediate repeats, incorporates edits, supports Previous, and restores its remaining order across sessions.
- Playback failures stay attached to queue entries, produce a themed notice, and offer retry, locate/relink, skip, or remove without hiding the rest of the queue.
- The bottom player includes an optional 30 FPS spectrum. Its read-only tap is disabled at zero analysis cost, preserves source bytes exactly, and does not analyze DoP packets.
- F11 and the bottom-player button enter a monitor-correct borderless fullscreen while retaining transport controls. Escape exits, and the short transition follows both app and Windows reduced-motion settings.

## Validation
